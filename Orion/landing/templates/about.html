About

Find the best spot to watch the sky, and bring your friends.

Orion is an astronomical event tracker and weather tracker in one. A great eclipse, meteor shower or satellite pass is only worth the drive if you can actually see it, so Orion shows where the event is visible and where the weather will cooperate. Then it helps you plan the trip with other people and see who can catch the most.

What we do

Live heatmap

See where it's actually visible

A heatmap combines where each event can be seen with live weather conditions, with AI working on real-time data to point you to the best spots.

Event tracker

Past, present and future

Browse a database of past events, follow the ones happening now, and look ahead to predictions for events still to come.

Community

Compete and plan together

Add friends, earn points for seeing events and livestreaming them, climb the leaderboard, and connect with others to plan trips.

How it works

  1. Pick an event

    Choose something happening now or coming up, or dig into the archive of past events.

  2. See the heatmap

    Visibility and live weather are combined into one map, so the best places to watch stand out at a glance.

  3. Plan the trip, then see it

    Invite friends or connect with other skywatchers, head out together, and earn points for seeing the event and livestreaming it.
